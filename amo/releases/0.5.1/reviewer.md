Version 0.5.1 moves optional Web Audio stereo panning out of live broadcast tabs and into the CHZZK clip editor window to avoid persistent AudioContext overhead and crackling on live tabs. The two previous live-broadcast Web Audio options are replaced by a single sub-option under keep-live-audio that pans the clip editor audio left or right (pan value ±0.4), while the live broadcast tab only uses native HTMLMediaElement.volume to reduce volume by 25% while the clip editor is open and restores the original volume on close. This release also reduces main-thread layout/style recalculation during typing, bottom chat layout updates, and player latency label rendering, and raises browser_specific_settings.gecko.strict_min_version to 142.0.

To test, grant CHZZK site access, open a live broadcast, and enable the keep-live-audio option and the clip-editor left/right panning sub-option in the popup. Open the clip editor with the broadcast's scissors button. Verify that the live broadcast remains audible at 75% of its previous volume without creating a Web Audio context on the live tab, that the clip editor audio pans left or right when toggled, and that closing the clip editor restores the original live broadcast volume and mute state.

The add-on ID, permissions, host access, and data collection declaration are unchanged. All 261 automated tests, repository checks, and web-ext lint passed. Live behavior on CHZZK was manually verified in Firefox, though playback and clip availability depend on the live CHZZK service.
