Version 0.5.1 moves optional Web Audio stereo panning from live broadcast tabs to the CHZZK clip editor window to avoid AudioContext overhead and crackling on live tabs. The two previous live-broadcast audio options are replaced by a single sub-option under keep-live-audio that pans clip editor audio left or right (±0.4), while the live tab only reduces native HTMLMediaElement.volume by 25% while the editor is open and restores it on close. This release also reduces main-thread layout/style recalculation during typing, bottom chat layout updates, and latency label rendering, and raises strict_min_version to 142.0.

To test, enable keep-live-audio and clip-editor panning in the popup, then open the clip editor from a live broadcast. Verify live audio stays audible at 75% volume, clip editor audio pans left/right when toggled, and closing the editor restores the original live volume and mute state.

Add-on ID, permissions, host access, and data collection declaration are unchanged. All 261 tests, repository checks, and web-ext lint passed, and live CHZZK behavior was manually verified in Firefox.
