CHZZK Catch-up helps reduce live playback latency on CHZZK and provides Firefox clip editor compatibility.

- Catch up when entering a broadcast or by clicking a button
- Automatic playback speed adjustment and buffer optimization
- Playback connection selection and latency/buffer indicators
- Separate switches for catch-up and clip editor compatibility

Requires Firefox 128 or later. Allow access to chzzk.naver.com and reload broadcast tabs after installation. Clip creation requires CHZZK sign-in and is subject to channel restrictions. Latency is an estimate; behavior depends on the broadcast and network.
